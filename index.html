About Me

I am currently a Ph.D. Candidate specializing in Biomedical Engineering and Aritificial Intelligence at the University of Alberta supervised by Dr. Jie Chen. My research focuses on leveraging AI and large language models for mental health diagnostics/intervention and Psychological Simulation (Digital Twin for Psychiatry).
